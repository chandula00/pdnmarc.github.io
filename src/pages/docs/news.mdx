The News page has two parts:

- **News articles**: full stories with their own page, written in Markdown in the
  [marc_news repository](https://github.com/pdnMARC/marc_news). Vacancies are news articles too.
- **Social feed items**: short entries that link out to a Facebook, LinkedIn or web post, added as rows
  in the website data Google Sheet.

```text
marc_news/
└── articles/
    ├── 2026-03-10-example-news.md
    └── cover_photos/
        └── 2026-03-10-example-news/
            ├── 01.jpg
            └── 02.jpg
```

<Callout title="Linking keys: keep these the same after publishing">

News articles are not linked to profiles or projects, so they are the easiest to edit. Two things still
act as keys:

- **The file name** is the article's address: `2026-03-10-example-news.md` becomes
  `/news/2026-03-10-example-news/`. Once an article is shared (on social media, in an email), don't rename
  the file, or those links stop working. Fix typos in the `title` instead.
- **Image paths** (`cover` and the images in the text) must match the photo files exactly, including
  upper and lower case. If you rename or move a photo, update every path that points to it.

Everything else (title, date, summary, text, vacancy fields) is safe to change at any time.

</Callout>

## Post a news article

1. Open the [`articles/`](https://github.com/pdnMARC/marc_news/tree/main/articles) folder and click
   **Add file → Create new file**.
2. Name the file after the date and a few words of the title, for example
   `2026-03-10-example-news.md`. This becomes the address: `/news/2026-03-10-example-news/`.
3. Upload photos to `articles/cover_photos/`, ideally in a folder named like the article.
4. Commit to a new branch and open a pull request. When it is merged, the website rebuilds.

```md
---
title: "MARC Researchers Win Best Paper Award"
date: 2026-03-10
cover: ./cover_photos/2026-03-10-example-news/01.jpg
short: "One or two sentences for the news card and the top of the article."
---

The full article, in Markdown.

## Photos

![The team receiving the award](./cover_photos/2026-03-10-example-news/02.jpg)
```

| Field | Required | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `title` | Yes | The headline. Must be unique across all articles. |
| `date` | Yes | Publication date, written exactly as `YYYY-MM-DD`. Articles are sorted by it, newest first. |
| `short` | Yes | The summary on the news card and under the headline. |
| `cover` | No | Cover photo, starting with `./cover_photos/`. Landscape, at least 1200 pixels wide. |

Describe every photo inside the square brackets (`![what the photo shows](…)`) so screen-reader users
know what it shows.

## Post a vacancy

A vacancy, internship or volunteer call is a news article with up to three extra fields:

```md
---
title: "Research Assistant: Hyperspectral Imaging"
date: 2026-10-20
short: "MARC is looking for a Research Assistant to join the hyperspectral imaging team."
vacancy: true
deadline: 2026-11-30
apply: https://forms.gle/your-form
---

The role, who can apply, and how to apply.
```

| Field | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `vacancy` | `true` lists the article on the [Vacancies](/vacancies/) page and tags it "Vacancy" in News. |
| `deadline` | Optional. Last day to apply, as `YYYY-MM-DD`. |
| `apply` | Optional. The application link: an `https://` address (a form, for example) or a `mailto:` address. Adds an **Apply** button. |

- The vacancy stays on the Vacancies page until the end of its `deadline` day (Sri Lanka time), then
  drops off by itself. The article stays in News and shows "Applications closed".
- Without a `deadline`, it stays on the Vacancies page until you remove `vacancy: true`.
- `deadline` and `apply` can only be used together with `vacancy: true`.

## Social feed items

Short updates that link to a post elsewhere go in the **News** tab of the website data Google Sheet
(ask a maintainer for edit access). Each row is one item in the "Latest activity" list on the News page.

| Column | Required | What to enter |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Title` | Yes | A short title for the post. |
| `Date` | Yes | `YYYY-MM-DD`. |
| `Source` | Yes | Exactly `Facebook`, `LinkedIn`, `Web` or `Twitter`. |
| `URL` | Yes | The full link to the post. |
| `Thumbnail` | No | Leave blank unless asked. |
| `Embed` | No | Leave blank unless asked. |

Rows missing a Title, Date or URL are skipped. New rows appear after the next website rebuild.

Double-check `Source`, `Date` and `URL`: a misspelled source, a date in another format or an incomplete
link stops the website from rebuilding until the row is fixed.

## If the check fails

When you open a pull request, an automatic check validates every article. If it fails, open the
**Checks** tab to see which file and field to fix. Common problems: a date not written as `YYYY-MM-DD`,
a title already used by another article, a `cover` path that does not match the file, or an article
saved outside the `articles/` folder.

More detail: the [marc_news README](https://github.com/pdnMARC/marc_news#readme).
