Each person at MARC keeps their own profile up to date. Profiles live in the
[marc_people repository](https://github.com/pdnMARC/marc_people) on GitHub: one Markdown file per person
in the `profiles/` folder, with photos in `profiles/photos/`.

```text
marc_people/
└── profiles/
    ├── Jane_Doe.md
    └── photos/
        └── jane_doe.jpg
```

<Callout title="Linking keys: how your profile connects to projects and publications">

- **Emails link you to projects.** Projects list their team by email address. The website matches each
  address to the profile whose `email`, **or any address in `otherEmails`**, is the same.
- **Names link you to publications.** A paper appears on your profile when its Authors column contains
  your `name`, **or any form in `otherNames`** (for example `Doe, J.` or `J. A. Doe`).

So list every address projects might use, and every form of your name used in your papers. Nothing then
drops off your profile, even when people write your address or name differently.
See [Other emails and names](#other-emails-and-names).

</Callout>

## Update your profile

You can do everything in the browser:

1. Open your file in [`profiles/`](https://github.com/pdnMARC/marc_people/tree/main/profiles) and click the pencil icon (**Edit this file**).
2. Sign in to GitHub if asked. If you are not a member of the repository, GitHub offers to fork it for you. Accept.
3. Change what you need. To replace your `email` or `name`, keep the old one in `otherEmails` or
   `otherNames` (see [Changing your email or name](#changing-your-email-or-name)).
4. Click **Commit changes…**, choose to create a new branch, then **Create pull request**.
5. When a MARC maintainer merges it, the website rebuilds and your changes go live.

Joined or left a project? That is changed in the project's file, not in your profile. See
[Add yourself to your projects](#add-yourself-to-your-projects).

To change your photo, first upload the new image to
[`profiles/photos/`](https://github.com/pdnMARC/marc_people/tree/main/profiles/photos)
(**Add file → Upload files**), then point `photo:` at it.

## Add a new profile

In the `profiles/` folder, click **Add file → Create new file**, name it after yourself (for example
`Jane_Doe.md`) and start from this template:

```md
---
email: jane.doe@eng.pdn.ac.lk
post: Research Assistant
name: Jane Doe
photo: ./photos/jane_doe.jpg
otherEmails:
  - e19999@eng.pdn.ac.lk
  - jane.doe@gmail.com
otherNames:
  - "Doe, J."
  - "J. A. Doe"
---
Jane Doe is a Research Assistant at MARC working on ...

## Research interests

- Computer vision
- Remote sensing
```

`otherEmails` and `otherNames` are optional: leave them out if you have nothing to add. Before you save,
check the linking keys:

- **`email`**: your main address. If you are already listed on a project, use the address in that project
  file, or add it to `otherEmails`.
- **`otherEmails`**: any other address a project file may list for you: a student address, an old
  address, a personal one.
- **`name`**: your name as you want it shown, for example `Jane Doe`.
- **`otherNames`**: every other way your name is written in the author lists of your papers.

Then [add yourself to your projects](#add-yourself-to-your-projects). Your profile does not say which
projects you are on, so that is a second, separate change.

## Other emails and names

People are often written down in more than one way: a student address and a staff address, or
`Jane Doe` in one paper and `Doe, J. A.` in another. List the extra forms so the website can connect them
all to you.

```yaml
otherEmails:
  - e19999@eng.pdn.ac.lk
otherNames:
  - "Doe, J."
  - "J. A. Doe"
  - "D. M. J. A. Doe"
```

- **Copy name forms from your papers' author lists.** Upper and lower case, full stops and spacing are
  ignored, so `J.A. Doe`, `J. A. Doe` and `j a doe` are the same form.
- **Make each name form specific to you.** It must have at least two words, and it must not match
  another MARC member: if a colleague could also be `Doe, J.`, use the longer form with all your
  initials. The automatic check stops two profiles from sharing a name form.
- **Each email belongs to one person.** The check stops an address from appearing in two profiles.
- Put quotes around name forms that contain a comma, as in the examples.
- Emails are never shown on the website, and `otherNames` are only used for matching.

## Add yourself to your projects

Each project file lists its own team; your profile does not. To appear on a project's page, and to have
the project appear on your profile, one of your addresses (`email` or any of `otherEmails`) must be in
that project's file in the [marc_projects repository](https://github.com/pdnMARC/marc_projects/tree/main/projects).

1. Find your project's file. Its name is in the project's page address: `/projects/inr/` is `INR.md`.
2. Open it in [`projects/`](https://github.com/pdnMARC/marc_projects/tree/main/projects) and click the pencil icon.
3. Add your email, **copied from your profile**, under `projectMembers` if you are a researcher or
   `supervisors` if you supervise the project.
4. Commit to a new branch and open a pull request in marc_projects.

If the list is empty (`[]`), replace the brackets with a list. Before:

```yaml
projectMembers: []
```

After:

```yaml
projectMembers:
  - jane.doe@eng.pdn.ac.lk
```

If it already has people, add one line for yourself at the end:

```yaml
projectMembers:
  - researcher1@eng.pdn.ac.lk
  - jane.doe@eng.pdn.ac.lk
```

Each line starts with two spaces, a dash and a space. Don't change anyone else's line.

- **Already listed under another address?** No need to change the project: add that address to your
  `otherEmails` instead.
- **Two pull requests.** Your profile (marc_people) and the project file (marc_projects) are in
  different repositories. They can be merged in either order: you appear on the project once both are
  merged.
- **Several projects?** Repeat the steps for each project file.
- **Leaving a project, or becoming a supervisor?** Remove your line from that list, or move it from
  `projectMembers` to `supervisors`, the same way.

## Fields

**Linking keys**: other content refers to these. Add to them freely; remove or replace a value only as
described in [Changing your email or name](#changing-your-email-or-name).

| Field | Required | Linked from | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `email` | Yes | `projectMembers` and `supervisors` in [project files](/docs/projects/) | Your main address and unique identifier. Never shown on the website. |
| `otherEmails` | No | The same project lists | Extra addresses projects may use for you, one per line. Each address may appear in only one profile. |
| `name` | Yes | The `Authors` column of the [publications sheet](/docs/publications/) | Your display name, and the first form used to find your papers. Titles such as Prof. or Dr. are ignored. |
| `otherNames` | No | The same Authors column | Other forms of your name in author lists, one per line, at least two words each. |

Email matching ignores upper and lower case, so `Jane.Doe@…` and `jane.doe@…` are the same address.

**Safe to change** at any time:

| Field | Required | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `post` | Yes | Your role. Use exactly one of `Director`, `Deputy Director`, `Research Assistant` or `Volunteer`. The People page groups people by role. |
| `photo` | No | Path to your photo, starting with `./photos/`. Without one, a neutral avatar is shown. |
| Biography | No | Everything below the closing `---`, in Markdown: paragraphs, headings, lists, links and images. If empty, the Biography section is not shown. |

The **file name** sets your page address: `Jane_Doe.md` becomes `/people/jane_doe/`. Nothing on the site
depends on it, but renaming the file changes the address, so links shared elsewhere (a CV, an email
signature) stop working.

## Changing your email or name

Never just delete an old address or name form: move it to the "other" list, and every existing link keeps
working without touching any project file or the publications sheet.

**Your main email changes.** Put the new address in `email` and move the old one to `otherEmails`:

```yaml
email: jane.doe@pdn.ac.lk
otherEmails:
  - jane.doe@eng.pdn.ac.lk
```

**Your name changes, or you want it shown differently.** Put the new form in `name` and move the old one
to `otherNames`:

```yaml
name: Jane Perera
otherNames:
  - "Jane Doe"
  - "Doe, J."
```

Remove an old value only when nothing uses it any more: no project file lists that address, and no
paper lists that form of your name.

## Photo tips

- Use a `.jpg`, `.jpeg` or `.png` file, ideally square and at least 400 × 400 pixels.
- Photos are shown in a circle, so keep your face near the centre and towards the top.
- The `photo` path must match the file name exactly, including upper and lower case: the website is
  built on a system where `Jane.JPG` and `jane.jpg` are different files.

## What appears on your profile

- **Associated projects**: every project whose `projectMembers` or `supervisors` list contains your
  `email` or one of your `otherEmails`. See [Projects](/docs/projects/).
- **Publications**: papers in the publications sheet whose Authors column contains your `name` or one of
  your `otherNames` as a whole name. See [Publications](/docs/publications/).

## If the check fails

When you open a pull request, an automatic check validates every profile. If it fails, open the
**Checks** tab of the pull request to see which file and field to fix. The most common problems:

- a `post` that is not one of the four roles;
- an address (in `email` or `otherEmails`) already used by another profile;
- a name form already used by another profile, or one with only one word;
- a `photo` path that does not match the file;
- a profile saved outside the `profiles/` folder.

More detail: the [marc_people README](https://github.com/pdnMARC/marc_people#readme).
