Each person at MARC keeps their own profile up to date. Profiles live in the
[marc_people repository](https://github.com/pdnMARC/marc_people) on GitHub: one Markdown file per person
in the `profiles/` folder, with photos in `profiles/photos/`.

```text
marc_people/
└── profiles/
    ├── Jane_Doe.md
    └── photos/
        └── jane_doe.jpg
```

<Callout title="Linking keys: keep these exactly the same">

Two values in your profile connect you to the rest of the site:

- **`email`** links you to **projects**. Projects list their team by email, and the website matches
  each address to the profile with exactly the same `email`.
- **`name`** links you to **publications**. A paper appears on your profile when its Authors column in
  the publications sheet contains this full name.

If either one changes without the other side being updated, your projects or papers quietly disappear
from your profile. See [Changing a linking key safely](#changing-a-linking-key-safely).

</Callout>

## Update your profile

You can do everything in the browser:

1. Open your file in [`profiles/`](https://github.com/pdnMARC/marc_people/tree/main/profiles) and click the pencil icon (**Edit this file**).
2. Sign in to GitHub if asked. If you are not a member of the repository, GitHub offers to fork it for you. Accept.
3. Change your biography or the safe-to-change fields. Leave `email` and `name` as they are.
4. Click **Commit changes…**, choose to create a new branch, then **Create pull request**.
5. When a MARC maintainer merges it, the website rebuilds and your changes go live.

To change your photo, first upload the new image to
[`profiles/photos/`](https://github.com/pdnMARC/marc_people/tree/main/profiles/photos)
(**Add file → Upload files**), then point `photo:` at it.

## Add a new profile

In the `profiles/` folder, click **Add file → Create new file**, name it after yourself (for example
`Jane_Doe.md`) and start from this template:

```md
---
email: jane.doe@eng.pdn.ac.lk
post: Research Assistant
name: Jane Doe
photo: ./photos/jane_doe.jpg
---
Jane Doe is a Research Assistant at MARC working on ...

## Research interests

- Computer vision
- Remote sensing
```

Before you save, check the two linking keys:

- **`email`**: the address your project leads use (or will use) in their project files. Write it in
  lower case. If you are already listed on a project, copy the address from that project file.
- **`name`**: your name as it appears in the author lists of your papers, for example `Jane Doe`.

## Fields

**Linking keys**: other content refers to these. Change them only as described below.

| Field | Required | Linked from | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `email` | Yes | `projectMembers` and `supervisors` in [project files](/docs/projects/) | Your unique identifier. Must be exactly the address projects use, letter for letter. Never shown on the website. |
| `name` | Yes | The `Authors` column of the [publications sheet](/docs/publications/) | Your display name, and how your papers are found. Titles such as Prof. or Dr. are ignored when matching. |

**Safe to change** at any time:

| Field | Required | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `post` | Yes | Your role. Use exactly one of `Director`, `Deputy Director`, `Research Assistant` or `Volunteer`. The People page groups people by role. |
| `photo` | No | Path to your photo, starting with `./photos/`. Without one, a neutral avatar is shown. |
| Biography | No | Everything below the closing `---`, in Markdown: paragraphs, headings, lists, links and images. If empty, the Biography section is not shown. |

The **file name** sets your page address: `Jane_Doe.md` becomes `/people/jane_doe/`. Nothing on the site
depends on it, but renaming the file changes the address, so links shared elsewhere (a CV, an email
signature) stop working.

## Changing a linking key safely

**Your email address changes.** Update both sides at the same time:

1. Find every project that lists your old address: search the
   [marc_projects repository](https://github.com/pdnMARC/marc_projects) on GitHub for it.
2. Open one pull request in marc_projects replacing the old address with the new one in each of those
   files, and one in marc_people changing your `email`.
3. Ask the maintainer to merge both together.

**Your name changes, or is written differently in your papers.** Papers are matched on the full name, so
keep `name` as it is written in your author lists. If you change it, papers listed under the old form of
your name no longer appear on your profile until their Authors entries use the new one.

## Photo tips

- Use a `.jpg`, `.jpeg` or `.png` file, ideally square and at least 400 × 400 pixels.
- Photos are shown in a circle, so keep your face near the centre and towards the top.
- The `photo` path must match the file name exactly, including upper and lower case: the website is
  built on a system where `Jane.JPG` and `jane.jpg` are different files.

## What appears on your profile

- **Associated projects**: every project whose `projectMembers` or `supervisors` list contains your
  `email`. See [Projects](/docs/projects/).
- **Publications**: papers in the publications sheet whose Authors column contains your full `name`.
  See [Publications](/docs/publications/).

## If the check fails

When you open a pull request, an automatic check validates every profile. If it fails, open the
**Checks** tab of the pull request to see which file and field to fix. The most common problems are a
`post` that is not one of the four roles, an `email` already used by another profile, a `photo` path
that does not match the file, or a profile saved outside the `profiles/` folder.

More detail: the [marc_people README](https://github.com/pdnMARC/marc_people#readme).
